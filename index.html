Why we're building this

From scattered records to one clear picture

Today

  • Evacuees listed on paper forms and separate spreadsheets
  • Center capacity passed around by word of mouth
  • Supplies counted by hand at each center
  • Families split across centers are hard to find
  • Officials piece together the situation from phone calls

With the system

  • Every evacuee registered once, grouped by family
  • Occupied and available spaces computed automatically
  • Food, water, kits, and blankets tracked per center
  • Search any evacuee or family across all centers
  • Live summaries and reports for decision-makers

What the system does

Built for the realities of an evacuation

A focused information and management tool. It supports the people running evacuation centers and never replaces their judgment.

Evacuee registration

Record name, age, contact, and assigned center in seconds at the registration desk.

Family grouping

Keep households together on record so family members are easy to locate.

Capacity monitoring

Occupied and remaining spaces update as evacuees are registered, with Open, Nearly Full, Full, and Closed indicators.

Supply tracking

Log food, water, hygiene kits, and blankets as they arrive and are distributed.

Staff & volunteers

Know who is assigned where and what each person is responsible for.

Emergency announcements

Post instructions and updates that staff, volunteers, and evacuees can all see.

Search & filtering

Find any evacuee, family, center, or supply without flipping through stacks of forms.

Reports & summaries

Totals for evacuees, occupancy, and supplies, ready for officials and relief workers.

Role-based access

Administrators, center staff, and volunteers each see only the tools their role needs.

Who it's for

One system, every role in the response

  • Barangay officials

    Monitor the overall situation and view reports on evacuees, centers, and resources.

  • Center coordinators & staff

    Register evacuees, update capacity, and manage their assigned center.

  • Volunteers

    Help with registration, record updates, and monitoring basic needs.

  • Health & relief workers

    See the evacuee and supply information they need to give assistance.

  • Evacuees & families

    Find open centers, available space, and important announcements.

How it works

Three steps, from arrival to overview

  1. 1

    Register on arrival

    Staff or volunteers record each evacuee and their family group at the center's desk.

  2. 2

    Capacity updates itself

    Occupied and available spaces recalculate, and the center's status changes when it fills up.

  3. 3

    Everyone sees the same picture

    Officials get summaries, evacuees see where to go, and announcements reach every center at once.

Lend a hand

Volunteer at an evacuation center

Registration desks, relief distribution, and record keeping all need extra hands during an emergency. Leave your details and a barangay coordinator will reach out.

  • Your details are only visible to barangay coordinators
  • You can choose the center closest to you
